Welcome to Noelle AI Studio

Noelle runs open-weight models from the Hugging Face Hub on this Mac: chat, vision, speech, image generation and embeddings. Prompts, documents and outputs stay on your computer.

This installer will:

  1. Install Noelle.app (about 1.5 GB).
  2. Open the Noelle Setup Assistant when it finishes, where you can:
    • add your Hugging Face access token (optional, stored in your Keychain),
    • check what your hardware can run,
    • choose optional downloads — nothing is downloaded unless you tick it.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Apple Silicon Macs run models on the GPU through Metal.
