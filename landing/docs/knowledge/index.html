Knowledge

Build local knowledge bases from your documents, chat with them with citations and measure retrieval quality.

Knowledge turns your documents into searchable knowledge bases that chat models can use to answer with citations. Everything — reading files, embeddings and search — happens on your computer.

Create a knowledge base#

  1. Open Knowledge and click New knowledge base.
  2. Give it a name and pick an embedding model. For English, sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 is small and fast; for Indonesian and other languages, BAAI/bge-m3 works well. If the model isn't downloaded yet, Noelle offers to download it.
  3. On the Documents tab, add files or a whole folder.

Supported files include PDF, Word (.docx), Markdown, plain text, HTML, CSV, JSON and source code. Hidden folders such as .git are skipped. Scanned PDFs without a text layer are reported as skipped.

Search#

The Search tab runs a query against the base and shows the best passages with their score, file and page, and a button to copy a citation.

Chat with your documents#

In the Workspace text playground, tick Documents and pick a knowledge base. Answers cite their sources, and the Sources panel shows the passages that were used. Tick Web search as well to add results from your own SearXNG instance (set its address in Settings › Grounding).

Measure retrieval quality#

The Evaluate tab checks whether the right documents come back for your questions. Add cases — a question and the file names (or parts of paths) that should be found — or import them as JSONL:

{"query": "How do I reset the router?", "relevant": ["router-manual.pdf"]}
{"query": "What is the refund period?", "relevant": ["policies/refunds.md"]}

Pick k (how many results count) and run. Noelle reports:

Metric Meaning
Hit rate@k Share of questions with at least one relevant document in the top k
Recall@k Share of the expected documents that were found
Precision@k Share of the top-k results that are relevant
MRR How high the first relevant result ranks, on average
nDCG@k Ranking quality, rewarding relevant results near the top

Select a second knowledge base to compare A vs B — for example the same documents with two chunk sizes or two embedding models.

Settings#

Each knowledge base has its own chunk size and overlap (512 and 64 tokens by default), used for documents you add afterwards. The embedding model is fixed once the base contains documents.
